Burn & Run

A simple game where you burn calories and run!

Instructions

  • Click "Start Game" to begin.
  • Enter your name when prompted.
  • Select a difficulty level: Easy or Hard.
  • Move up and down to dodge the fireballs: arrow keys on a keyboard, or drag in the arena / use the buttons on a touch screen.
  • Survive and score before the timer runs out to win!
